Common questions

Where is my data stored?

On your phone only. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded. See the privacy policy.

Will my sessions move to a new phone?

Only if your device backup or transfer includes app data. Sessions aren't synced through any account.

The click is silent when my ringer is off.

Dog Clicker is designed to play with the ringer switch off. Check the app's volume in Settings and your phone's media volume. If it's still silent, email us.
