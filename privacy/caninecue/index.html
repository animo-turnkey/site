CanineCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how CanineCue (the "app"), made by Animo Network Strategies LLC ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Summary

CanineCue does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your data. Your training sessions and everything else you create in the app stay on your device. The only outside service the app uses is RevenueCat, which handles the optional one-time Pro purchase (see In-app purchases).

No accounts

You don't sign up or sign in to use CanineCue. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Training sessions you save (start time, duration, rep count, and skill)
  • Custom skill names you add
  • Your settings, such as click sound, volume, and vibration

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a session in the app removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backups) may include app data according to your device settings; those backups are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Data we collect

None. CanineCue contains no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking. The only third-party SDK is RevenueCat, used for the Pro purchase as described below. The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

In-app purchases

CanineCue offers an optional one-time Pro upgrade. Payment is handled by Apple through the App Store; we never see your payment details.

We use RevenueCat to process and verify the Pro purchase and to restore it on a new device. To do this, the app connects to RevenueCat when it opens to check whether Pro is unlocked, and when you buy or restore Pro. RevenueCat receives:

  • An anonymous app user ID that RevenueCat generates for your install. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple ID.
  • Purchase details from Apple, such as the product bought, the transaction ID, and the purchase date.
  • Basic technical information needed to make the connection, such as your IP address, device model, operating system version, app version, and country or currency.

RevenueCat does not receive your name, email address, or your training data. RevenueCat handles this information under its own privacy policy.

Advertising

CanineCue shows no ads.

Children's privacy

CanineCue is not directed at children under 13, and because the app collects no personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect in the app.

Contact
