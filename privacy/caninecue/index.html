CanineCue Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy explains how CanineCue (the "app"), made by Animo Network Strategies LLC ("we", "us"), handles your information.

Summary

CanineCue does not collect personal data. There are no accounts, and we run no servers that receive your data. Everything you create in the app stays on your device.

No accounts

You don't sign up or sign in to use CanineCue. We don't ask for your name, email address, phone number, or any other contact details.

Data stored on your device

The app saves the following only on your phone, in the app's private storage:

  • Training sessions you save (start time, duration, rep count, and skill)
  • Custom skill names you add
  • Your settings, such as click sound, volume, and vibration

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Deleting a session in the app removes it, and uninstalling the app removes all of this data from your device. Your device's own backups (for example iCloud or Google backups) may include app data according to your device settings; those backups are handled by Apple or Google, not by us.

Data we collect

None. The current version of CanineCue contains no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. It does not use your location, contacts, camera, or microphone.

In-app purchases

Reserved for a later version. CanineCue does not currently offer in-app purchases. A future version may offer an optional one-time Pro upgrade processed through the App Store or Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. This section will be updated before that version is released to describe what purchase information is processed and by whom.

Advertising

Reserved for a later version. CanineCue does not currently show ads. A future version may show a single banner ad from Google AdMob on secondary screens. This section will be updated before that version is released to describe what data AdMob may collect, your choices about personalized ads, and how to opt out.

Children's privacy

CanineCue is not directed at children under 13, and because the app collects no personal data, we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles data, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above before the change takes effect in the app.

Contact

Questions about this policy? See the support page for how to reach us.
